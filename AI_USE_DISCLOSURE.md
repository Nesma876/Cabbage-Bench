# AI use disclosure register

## Claude (Anthropic)

Claude assisted with development, refinement and debugging of scientific
analysis code and with computational procedures implemented in that code.
The authors specified the methods, reviewed the retained code, checked its
outputs against the experimental result files and remain responsible for the
analyses. Claude did not write the manuscript and did not independently
validate the science.

Exact Claude model/version and dates of use: **to be confirmed by the
authors before submission**.

## ChatGPT (OpenAI)

ChatGPT assisted with editing and reformulating author-supplied manuscript
text, scientific-English refinement, restructuring, condensation, section
organisation, LaTeX formatting and editorial-consistency checks. The authors
reviewed and approved the retained text and remain responsible for the final
article, interpretations, figures and tables.

Exact ChatGPT model/version and dates of use: **to be confirmed by the
authors before submission**.

## Editorial-policy status

BIB permits assistance with code writing and language correction and requires
all acceptable LLM use affecting article content to be declared in the cover
letter/manuscript and documented in the supplementary material. BIB also says
that drafting an article from prompt text is unacceptable. Because the
confirmed ChatGPT use includes substantive restructuring and condensation,
compliance cannot be asserted solely from the available record. The authors
should obtain a factual clarification from the editorial office before
submission.
