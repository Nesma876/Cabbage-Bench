# Cover letter draft -- CABBAGE-Bench

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, “CABBAGE-Bench: Evaluating Genomic
Antimicrobial Resistance Prediction under Population, Geographic and Temporal
Shifts,” for publication in *Briefings in Bioinformatics*.

The study introduces a reproducible evaluation framework rather than a new
prediction architecture. It defines fixed random, lineage-aware, geographic
and temporal protocols across six bacterial species and examines how genomic
representation, population structure, geography, time and phenotype standard
affect antimicrobial-resistance prediction.

We disclose the use of AI-assisted tools in preparing this submission. Claude
(Anthropic) assisted with development, refinement and debugging of
computational analysis code. ChatGPT (OpenAI) assisted with editing and
reformulating author-supplied text, scientific-English refinement,
restructuring, condensation, section organisation and LaTeX formatting. The
authors designed the study, made all methodological and interpretive
decisions, reviewed retained code and text, verified reported results against
archived outputs and take full responsibility for the work. Exact model
versions and dates of use remain to be inserted before submission.

The benchmark artefacts will be archived in one versioned Zenodo record and
the data and software citation will be inserted after its DOI is public and
verified.
